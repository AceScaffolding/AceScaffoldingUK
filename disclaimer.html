This website is a public reference point for standards, communication awareness, opportunity discipline and information-handling expectations.

It is deliberately clear about its limits. Public wording is useful, but it is not a substitute for proper agreements, controlled documents, compliance checks or legal advice where risk matters.

Plain limits

What This Website Cannot Do

No automatic legal agreement

Viewing this website does not automatically create a contract, confidentiality agreement or legally binding communication arrangement.

No legal advice

This website is not legal advice. Professional advice should be obtained where rights, obligations or risk exposure need to be assessed.

No visitor identity verification

This website does not verify who is viewing it and does not confirm authorisation to receive confidential information.

No private access control

This public static website does not provide a private portal, personalised access codes or true visitor authentication.

No private arrangements published

Phone numbers, call times, meeting dates, meeting locations and private arrangements are handled individually, not published here.

No service advertising

This website does not advertise construction, scaffolding, engineering, site work or contracting services.

No authorisation of unlawful activity

This website does not authorise illegal activity, restricted goods, stolen property, misrepresentation, fraud, unsafe activity or unauthorised dealing.

No guarantee of opportunity

Nothing on this website guarantees access to work, clients, buyers, suppliers, products, introductions, arrangements or commercial opportunities.

Platform limits

GitHub Pages Limitations

GitHub Pages is suitable for a public static information hub. It should not be treated as a secure document room, private member area or proof of authorisation.

  • It cannot require personalised visitor authentication codes.
  • It cannot create a private member portal.
  • It cannot verify visitor identity.
  • It cannot provide legal confidentiality protection by itself.
  • It cannot prevent visitors from copying public webpage content.
  • It cannot provide true visitor 2FA authentication.

Recommended controls

  • Enable GitHub account 2FA.
  • Restrict repository collaborator access.
  • Use a strong GitHub password.
  • Use GitHub Pages HTTPS.
  • Do not upload confidential documents to the public repository.
  • Use contracts, confidentiality agreements, employment agreements, supplier agreements and document controls for stronger protection.
Conduct limit

Lawful, Authorised and Honest Activity Only

Ace Scaffolding UK uses confidentiality to protect legitimate relationships, information and standards. It does not use confidentiality to conceal unlawful conduct or to give anyone authority they do not have.

If a person is unsure whether they may hold, share, request, supply, introduce, sell, buy or discuss something, they should stop and clarify authority before acting.
