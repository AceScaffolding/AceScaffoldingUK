Confidentiality policy

Confidentiality Policy

This policy sets expectations for business information, contacts, leads, internal messages, opportunity details and sensitive operational or commercial material.

Confidentiality is protected by behaviour, agreements and controls. A public website can state the standard, but it cannot replace the documents and processes that enforce it.

Access discipline

Authorised Access Principles

1

Access should be limited to people with a legitimate reason to receive the information.

2

Recipients should check the classification and intended audience before forwarding, copying, capturing or discussing material.

3

Confidential information should not be placed in public channels, public repositories or uncontrolled shared spaces.

4

Where uncertainty exists, the correct response is to pause, clarify permission and use an authorised route.

Classification

Classification Awareness

PUBLIC

Approved for public release.

INTERNAL

Intended for Ace Scaffolding UK members and authorised partners only.

CONFIDENTIAL

Contains confidential business, contact, lead, opportunity or operational information. Do not share without permission.

RESTRICTED

Sensitive operational or commercial information. Distribution is limited to specifically authorised recipients.

STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

Highly sensitive information. Copying, forwarding, reproduction or disclosure without written authorisation is prohibited except where required by law.

Expected standard

Confidentiality Expectations

Recipients should protect business information, client or contact information, correspondence records, internal decisions, prices, routes, introductions, opportunity details and any material marked as confidential, restricted or strictly confidential.

  • Do not copy, forward, capture, reproduce or disclose confidential material without permission, except where required by law.
  • Do not use confidential information for a purpose outside the reason it was shared.
  • Do not move sensitive material into public repositories, public webpages or uncontrolled chat groups.
  • Report misdirected or wrongly received confidential information promptly.
  • Do not expose contacts, leads, pricing, sources, routes or internal discussions without authority.

Confidentiality obligations may also come from contracts, confidentiality agreements, employment terms, supplier agreements and law.

Legal boundary

Confidentiality Has Limits

Confidentiality is used to protect legitimate information, relationships, opportunities and internal standards. It must never be used to hide unlawful conduct, misrepresentation, fraud, stolen property, restricted goods, unsafe activity or unauthorised dealing.

Rule: if something appears unlawful, restricted, unsafe, wrongly obtained or outside authority, stop, do not circulate it and escalate through the appropriate channel.
